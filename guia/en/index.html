In short

Here are two ways to chat with AI on Telegram while it works on your computer (which can be a server: a computer that stays on all the time, at home or rented). The first is Claude Code remote control (Claude Code is Anthropic’s assistant that works on your computer, through your Claude subscription): a bot checks that it’s you and passes your request to Claude Code, which replies or carries it out. The second is Intelecto’s Jarvis: an assistant with an identity, memory, rules, and safeguards. It uses AI as its brain, and you can change that brain. It’s for anyone who wants to send tasks from their phone without opening their computer. The project includes real demonstrations, animated diagrams, and videos.

The benefit

The work happens on your computer. You only need the chat.

Today, to ask AI running on your computer to do anything, you need to sit in front of it.

😩 Before

You remember something while out (“How much disk space is left?”), but can only check when you get home and open your computer. And if you ask for something that deletes files, it all depends on whether AI remembers to ask first.

😌 After

Once the Telegram bot is set up, send your question and the answer arrives in the same chat in seconds. If your request deletes, changes, or sends something outside, AI shows what it plans to do and waits for your “yes.”

1

From your phone, anywhere

Telegram becomes the way in and out. No need to sit at your computer.

2

Only you can send requests

Reading and checking, it does right away. Before deleting, changing, or sending, it asks. In Jarvis, this is a lock in the code. In the remote control, it is a rule the AI is given (and it followed it in the test).

How it works, in plain language

Option 1: four parts, like a message handed over in person

Think of a message you leave at the front desk of a building.

1

Telegram

The front desk: where you leave your message and get the reply.

2

Bot

The doorman: checks that it’s you before passing your message along.

3

Claude Code

The staff member who understands your request and decides what to do.

4

Terminal

The engine room: the command line, where the computer carries out instructions.

Option 2: Jarvis has 8 parts, each with a purpose

The brain thinks. The other seven make sure it works for you, your way, and safely.

1

Channel

The building intercom with a resident list: it only replies to people on the list.

2

Loop with a limit

A limit on attempts: at most 5 rounds, so it never runs endlessly.

3

Identity

The ID badge and handbook: who it is, how it works, and who you are, in text you can edit.

4

Brain

The car engine: the AI that thinks. It can run on your computer, in the cloud, or be a test version. Changing the engine doesn’t change the rest.

5

Memory

The notebook: saves what you ask it to remember and finds it later, even without accent marks.

6

Tools

The hands: read and save files in the work folder, and run commands.

7

Safeguard and vault

The security guard at the door: reading is allowed, changing requires your OK, and destroying is never allowed. Passwords are kept in a locked vault, and everything is written in a logbook (the audit trail).

8

Sandbox

A closed room without internet (a Docker container): if something goes wrong, the damage stays inside.

Questions

Common questions

What do I need?

A computer that stays on all the time (or a rented server), a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, and Telegram on your phone.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Paste the prompt into Claude Code or Codex, and it sets things up with you, explaining each step.

Can someone control my server through the bot?

No. The bot is set up to accept only your user ID. Anyone not on the list gets “access denied” (tested on the demonstration screen).

Does it cost anything?

The project is free. Option 1 uses your Claude subscription. Option 2 can use AI running on your own computer (no cloud costs, but it needs a powerful computer) or cloud AI.

Which one should I choose?

Start with Option 1 to see it working. When you want memory, your own rules, and the ability to switch AI, move to Jarvis.
